# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to help engineers get better at their craft and prepare
for real interviews. Participation — issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews — is expected to stay focused on that goal and to be conducted
respectfully.

**Expected:**

- Give technically substantive feedback, especially on correctness.
- Disagree on technical grounds, not personal ones.
- Assume good faith on first contact; escalate to maintainers rather than
  engaging in a public back-and-forth if a disagreement isn't resolving.
- Credit sources appropriately (official docs, RFCs, standards bodies) when
  a contribution draws on them.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language/behavior of any
  kind.
- Submitting content you know to be plagiarized, copied, or containing
  real confidential/employer information, after being told not to.
- Bad-faith reviews intended to block contributions rather than improve
  them.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other official communication channel for this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
don't meet this standard, and may block repeat or severe violations from
further participation. Report concerns by opening an issue or contacting a
maintainer directly if the concern involves another contributor's conduct.
