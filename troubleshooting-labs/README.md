## What this topic covers

This folder focuses on troubleshooting labs through the lens of how production systems really behave. The material is organized so a learner can move from mechanism-level understanding into debugging, architecture, and leadership judgment without switching formats or naming conventions.

## Focus areas

`symptoms, signals, hypotheses, evidence, remediation, postmortems`
