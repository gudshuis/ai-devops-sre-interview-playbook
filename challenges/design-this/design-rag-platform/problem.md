# Problem

Your organization has ~40 internal teams, each with their own document
sets (runbooks, design docs, policy documents, customer data — varying
sensitivity). Several teams have already built their own one-off RAG
integrations against different vector databases with inconsistent access
control, and it's become clear this needs to be a shared platform instead
of 40 bespoke implementations.

Design a **RAG platform** that any of these 40 teams can onboard their
documents to, with correct per-user authorization, without every team
needing to become RAG-infrastructure experts themselves.
